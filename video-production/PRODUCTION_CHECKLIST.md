## 🚨 Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

### Pitfall 1: File Size Too Large
**Problem:** Instagram rejects files over 50MB  
**Solution:** 
- Use H.264 codec (not ProRes)
- Reduce bitrate to 8 Mbps
- Use HandBrake to compress without quality loss

### Pitfall 2: Text Unreadable on Mobile
**Problem:** Text too small or low contrast  
**Solution:**
- Minimum font size: 32px
- Always use white text with dark shadow
- Test on actual phone before publishing

### Pitfall 3: Video Looks "AI-Generated"
**Problem:** Uncanny valley, obvious AI artifacts  
**Solution:**
- Mix 40% AI + 60% real footage
- Use AI for B-roll, real recordings for UI
- Shorter AI clips (2-3s) look more realistic

### Pitfall 4: Audio Out of Sync
**Problem:** SFX don't match actions  
**Solution:**
- Zoom into timeline for frame-accurate placement
- Use markers to note SFX timing
- Export audio separately for testing

### Pitfall 5: Brand Colors Look Different
**Problem:** Purple looks pink or blue across clips  
**Solution:**
- Color grade all clips together
- Use color reference chart
- Export test frames and check hex values in Photoshop

### Pitfall 6: Posting at Wrong Time
**Problem:** Low engagement despite good video  
**Solution:**
- Post Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM IST
- Avoid weekends and late nights
- Use Instagram Insights to find YOUR audience's peak time

## ✨ Final Tips for Success

1. **Start Simple:** Your first version doesn't need to be perfect. Ship it, learn, iterate.

2. **Test Early:** Show rough cut to 3 people before final render. Fresh eyes catch issues.

3. **Mobile First:** 90% of Instagram users are on mobile. Test on phone BEFORE publishing.

4. **Hook is King:** First 3 seconds determine if people keep watching. Make it compelling.

5. **Captions Matter:** 85% watch without sound. Text overlays are essential.

6. **Engage Immediately:** Reply to comments in first hour = more reach from algorithm.

7. **Repurpose Content:** One video = Instagram Reel + Story + LinkedIn + YouTube Shorts + Website embed.

8. **Track & Learn:** Use data to improve next video. What worked? What didn't?
