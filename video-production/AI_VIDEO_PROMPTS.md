Use these across ALL prompts for consistency:

```

- Color Grading: Slightly desaturated, corporate clean
- Lighting: Soft, even, professional
```

```
- Movement: Smooth, slow, cinematic
- No: Shaky cam, fast zooms, jarring cuts
- Speed: 2-4 seconds per major movement
- Transitions: Ease-in-out curves
```

```
- Professional, confident, modern

- Tech-forward, innovative
- Clean, organized, premium
```

## ⚠️ Common AI Video Generation Issues & Fixes

### Issue: Generated video looks "AI-like" or uncanny
**Fix:** 
- Add "photorealistic", "professional video", "cinematic" to prompts
- Use higher quality settings
- Shorter clips (3-5s) look more realistic
- Mix AI footage with real screen recordings

### Issue: Brand colors don't match
**Fix:**
- Specify exact hex codes in prompt: "purple #5E3BE8"
- Color correct in post-production
- Upload reference image with your colors

### Issue: Camera movement is jerky
**Fix:**
- Specify "smooth", "slow", "gimbal-style" in prompt
- Extend generation time for smoother interpolation
- Stabilize in post with warp stabilizer

### Issue: Text on screen is illegible
**Fix:**
- Don't rely on AI to generate readable text
- Add all text overlays in post-production using After Effects/Premiere
- Use clean sans-serif fonts (Inter, Poppins)

### Issue: Duration is too short
**Fix:**
- Generate multiple versions and blend
- Use Runway's "Extend" feature
- Slow down footage slightly (95% speed)

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Mix Real + AI
Don't make it 100% AI-generated. Mix:
- 40% AI-generated B-roll (developer workspace, product reveals)
- 40% Real screen recordings (your actual app)
- 20% Graphics/text overlays (After Effects)

### Tip 2: Consistency Is Key
- Generate all shots in same session
- Use same style keywords across prompts
- Upload reference images for style matching
- Color grade everything together in post

### Tip 3: Start With Keyframes
Before generating video:
1. Create static mockups in Photoshop
2. Upload as reference images
3. Prompt AI to animate from that keyframe
4. Better consistency and control

### Tip 4: Budget Approach
If AI video tools are expensive:
- Use Canva Pro (has video templates)
- Stock footage from Pexels/Pixabay (free)
- Focus budget on music licensing
- DIY screen recordings (free)
