# Saheb Paper ERP — Launch Video Plan

## Overview & Creative Direction
- **Input:** Saheb Paper ERP codebase (Production-grade React 19 + TypeScript paper mill ERP)
- **Target Audience:** Paper mill executives, industrial plant managers, factory operators, and tech leaders
- **Tone:** Default (Punchy, modern tech energy, high-velocity shop floor rhythm)
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical (1080 × 1920), 30 fps
- **Duration:** 20.0 seconds (600 frames total)
- **Music Grid:** 120 BPM (0.50s per beat / 15 frames per beat / 30 frames per bar; exactly 40 beats)

## Core Answers
1. **What is it?** A specialized, end-to-end industrial manufacturing ERP engineered exclusively for kraft, duplex, and tissue paper mills.
2. **Who is it for, and what does it do?** Paper mill directors and shop floor teams; digitizes raw material inwards, hydrapulper chemistry, machine rolls, rewinder slitting, QR traceability, and dispatch gate passes.
3. **What sets it apart?** 100% QR-lineage traceability across every reel, touch-optimized shop floor UI, multi-role simulation, and offline-first zero-latency sync.
4. **The visual hook:** Instant transition from dusty physical paper logbooks to a sleek, dark-slate glassmorphism cockpit tracking a live 480 MPM paper machine.
5. **Real UI shown:** Executive Dashboard KPIs, Live Parent Jumbo Roll telemetry, Rewinder slitter with QR thermal sticker generation, QC Lab burst/tear test grade, and Dispatch Gate Pass.
6. **Punchline / Outro:** "Stop running a 50-crore paper mill on a 5-rupee notebook." -> "Saheb Paper ERP: Built for the mill. Engineered for scale."
7. **One-line share caption:** Meet the ERP built exclusively for paper mills: from raw pulp recipes to thermal QR reel dispatch in one unified touch system.
