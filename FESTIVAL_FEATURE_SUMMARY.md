# Saheb Paper - Festival Theme Feature

## Automatic Hindu Festival Login Themes

### Feature Highlights

**Zero Configuration Required**
- Automatically detects calendar date
- Applies appropriate Hindu festival theme
- No admin panel needed
- No manual updates required

### Supported Hindu Festivals (11 Themes)

1. **Makar Sankranti / Uttarayan** - January 14
   - Colorful kites and harvest celebration
   - Cyan, orange, yellow color scheme

2. **Maha Shivratri** - February 26
   - Lord Shiva devotional theme
   - Deep purple, blue, golden colors

3. **Holi** - March 14
   - Festival of colors celebration
   - Pink, purple, orange vibrant colors

4. **Ram Navami** - April 6
   - Lord Rama theme
   - Orange, red, golden colors

5. **Hanuman Jayanti** - April 13
   - Strength and devotion theme
   - Red, orange, golden yellow colors

6. **Krishna Janmashtami** - August 26
   - Lord Krishna joyful celebration
   - Royal blue, golden, purple colors

7. **Ganesh Chaturthi** - September 7
   - Ganpati Bappa Morya
   - Saffron orange, pink, green colors

8. **Navratri** - October 1-10
   - Goddess Durga and Garba celebration
   - Deep red, orange, golden colors

9. **Dussehra** - October 10
   - Victory of good over evil
   - Orange, red, golden colors

10. **Diwali** - October 20 - November 5
    - Festival of Lights
    - Deep orange, golden, bright yellow colors

11. **Default Theme** - All other days
    - Saheb Paper corporate purple theme
    - Professional business aesthetic
